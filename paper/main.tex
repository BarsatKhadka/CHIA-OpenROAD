\hline
Identical results for different knobs &
\texttt{make} does not invalidate on variable change; \texttt{make -n} reports
``all up to date'' even for a knob that does not exist. 12 candidates reported
a floorplan none had requested. \\
Prediction for a design that will not exist &
A \texttt{place}-stage model reads the \emph{shared} placement; any pre-CTS

LVS certified clean &

Iteration stalled 3.5\,h &
One unroutable floorplan; \texttt{run\_stage} defaulted to a 24\,h timeout. \\
